    <h2 id="focus-title">Learning under change</h2>
    <p>I develop methods that make machine learning systems more adaptive, efficient, and useful in evolving environments.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="focus-grid">
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-card__number">01</span>
      <h3>Continual learning</h3>
      <p>Adapting model structure and representations across sequential tasks while limiting catastrophic forgetting.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-card__number">02</span>
      <h3>Efficient adaptation</h3>
      <p>Using low-rank experts and probabilistic structure inference to add capability without retraining everything.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-card__number">03</span>

      <p>Applying adaptive learning to recommendation, generalized category discovery, and bioinformatics graphs.</p>
